NetHack for the KIM-1 -- commands

Movement:  y k u     Shift+direction (Y K U H L B J N) runs.
            \|/      A number before s or . repeats it (20s).
           h-.-l     Moving into a monster attacks it; moving into
            /|\      your pet swaps places with it.
           b j n

  ,  pick up everything here       d  drop an item
  i  show inventory                :  look at what is here
  w  wield a weapon (- for none)   W  wear armor
  T  take off armor                q  quaff (drink) a potion
  r  read a scroll                 e  eat something
  z  zap a wand                    s  search for hidden traps
  .  rest one turn                 E  engrave in the dust
  <  go up stairs                  >  go down stairs
  #pray  ask your god for help (only when in real trouble)
  S  save the game to the SD card and stop
  Q  quit without saving           O  toggle ANSI screen clearing
  ?  this help                     Ctrl-R  redraw the screen

Symbols:
  @ you       f your kitten     - | walls     . floor     # corridor
  + door      < > stairs        ^ trap        $ gold      ) weapon
  [ armor     ! potion          ? scroll      / wand      % food
  " the Amulet of Yendor        letters are monsters

Your quest: find the Amulet of Yendor on dungeon level 10 and carry it
back up the stairs from level 1.

Tips:
  Potions, scrolls and wands start unidentified; using one may tell
  you what it is.  Some armor and weapons are cursed and cannot be
  removed.  Eat before you faint.  Do not melee the floating eye.
  Monsters will not attack you in melee while you stand on a square
  engraved with Elbereth, but attacking from it rubs it out.
  Levels you leave are saved to the SD card, so they are still there
  when you come back.  Saving with S also uses the card; the save is
  removed when you restore it.
